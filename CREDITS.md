# Credits

Computah's code is original (MIT). Its art, sounds and screen font come from other projects:

## OpenComputers

Textures, GUI images and the floppy/drive sounds are from [OpenComputers](https://github.com/MightyPirates/OpenComputers)
by Florian "Sangar" Nücke and contributors. OpenComputers' images, textures and localization strings are public
domain (CC0 1.0 Universal); its licence text is kept with the assets at
`src/main/resources/assets/computah/LICENSE-OC-ASSETS`. Contributors credited by OpenComputers for these assets:

- PixelToast – capacitor textures.
- asie – disk drive inject/eject and floppy disk access sound samples.

## Hard drive sound

The hard drive access sounds (`hdd_access1-7.ogg`) are OpenComputers' modified versions of this sample:

> hard_drive_noise (miroslawski.net).wav by artykris -- https://freesound.org/s/117401/ -- License: Sampling+

The Sampling+ licence: "You are free to sample, mash-up, or otherwise creatively transform this sound (even for

as long as you credit the author and do not use the sound to advertise for or promote anything but the sound you
create from it."

## Screen font

The screen font (`font.hex`) is **unscii** by viznut, extended by asie (public domain), as distributed with
OpenComputers: https://github.com/asiekierka/unscii-asie

## Lua

Lua runs on [Cobalt](https://github.com/cc-tweaked/Cobalt) (MIT), bundled in the jar.
